Small trust/status marker — CardHearth's "fair play" voice in a chip.

```jsx
<Badge tone="positive">✓ Every deal winnable</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold">⭐ Daily Challenge</Badge>
<Badge tone="streak" icon="🔥">5-day streak</Badge>
```

- `positive` (mint, used for winnability promises), `gold` (eyebrow), `chip` (dark pill), `streak` (gold pill).

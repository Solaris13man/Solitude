[Spider](/spider/) and [FreeCell](/freecell/) are both wildly popular, but
they sit at opposite ends of the solitaire spectrum. The quickest way to tell them apart: FreeCell
shows you everything and is almost always winnable, while Spider hides cards, uses two decks, and
leaves some deals genuinely unbeatable. Here's how that plays out.

## How they're dealt

**FreeCell** deals all 52 cards *face up* into eight cascades, plus four free
cells as workspace. There's no stock and nothing hidden — the whole puzzle is in front of you from
move one. **Spider** uses *two* decks (104 cards) across ten columns, most of
them face down, with a stock that deals a new row whenever you're stuck. You don't know what you're
sitting on until you uncover it.

## The goal

In FreeCell you build the four foundations Ace-to-King by suit, using alternating-colour stacking
in the cascades along the way. In Spider you don't build foundations at all — you assemble complete
King-to-Ace runs *in a single suit* right in the columns, and each finished run is removed.
Clear all eight runs and you win. That single-suit rule is what makes Spider's difficulty scale so
sharply with the suit count.

## Difficulty and winnability

This is the biggest practical difference. FreeCell is **almost always winnable** — of
the classic 32,000 numbered deals only [#11982](/freecell/deal/11982/) has no solution —
so a loss is nearly always a misplay you can learn from. Spider's deals are **not screened for
solvability**: one-suit is forgiving, but [two suits](/spider-2-suits/) and
especially [four suits](/spider-4-suits/) include deals that cannot be won no matter how
you play. Our [are solitaire games winnable?](/guides/are-solitaire-games-winnable/) guide
has the full breakdown.

## Which should you play?

Choose **FreeCell** for a calm, fair, plan-it-all-out puzzle where skill is everything —
it's the best game on the site for getting genuinely better. Choose **Spider** when you
want a longer, more atmospheric grind with the tension of hidden cards and the luck of the draw.
Many players keep both in rotation: FreeCell to sharpen up, Spider to unwind. Dig deeper with the
[FreeCell strategy guide](/guides/freecell-strategy/) and the
[Spider strategy guide](/guides/spider-solitaire-strategy/), or compare FreeCell with the
other classic in [FreeCell vs Klondike](/guides/freecell-vs-klondike/).

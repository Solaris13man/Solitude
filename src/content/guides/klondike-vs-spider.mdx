[Klondike](/klondike/) is the solitaire most people picture when they hear the word —
the one that shipped with Windows. [Spider](/spider/) is the other heavyweight: a
two-deck game that trades Klondike's quick rounds for a longer, more absorbing build. They share
DNA (stacking cards in descending order) but they play very differently.

## Rules at a glance

**Klondike** uses one deck across seven columns, with a stock you turn either
[one card (Draw 1) or three at a time (Draw 3)](/guides/klondike-draw-1-vs-draw-3/). You
build four foundations from Ace to King by suit, stacking columns down in alternating colours.
**Spider** uses two decks across ten columns and no separate foundations — you build
King-to-Ace runs *in a single suit* directly in the columns, and each completed run lifts
off. When you're stuck, the stock deals a fresh card onto every column at once.

## Length and feel

Klondike is a **sprint**: one deck, quick to set up, quick to resolve, easy to play a
few rounds back to back. Spider is a **marathon**: twice the cards, more columns to
track, and a slower march toward those eight suit-runs. If you've got five minutes, Klondike fits;
if you want to sink into something, Spider rewards the time.

## Difficulty and winnability

Neither game screens its deals, so both include hands that simply can't be won. Klondike Draw 1 is
the gentler end (around 80% of deals are theoretically winnable); Draw 3 is tougher. Spider scales
with its suit count: one suit is forgiving, [two](/spider-2-suits/) is a real challenge,
and [four](/spider-4-suits/) is genuinely hard. For the honest, game-by-game picture,
see [are solitaire games winnable?](/guides/are-solitaire-games-winnable/)

## Which should you play?

Reach for **Klondike** when you want the familiar classic in bite-size rounds, and
**Spider** when you want a longer, suit-focused puzzle to get lost in. Level up with the
[how to win Klondike](/guides/how-to-win-klondike-solitaire/) and
[Spider strategy](/guides/spider-solitaire-strategy/) guides — or, if you'd rather a game
where almost every deal is winnable, try [Spider vs FreeCell](/guides/spider-vs-freecell/)
next.

## Solitaire (card games)

### [Klondike Solitaire](/klondike/)

The one most people mean by "Solitaire." Move every card to the foundations, building alternating-colour runs along the way. Forgiving, endlessly replayable, and the best place to start. New to it? Read [how to win Klondike](/guides/how-to-win-klondike-solitaire/).

### [Spider Solitaire](/spider/)

Build same-suit runs from King down to Ace across ten columns. The 2-suit game is a great middle challenge; 4-suit is for experts. Our [Spider strategy guide](/guides/spider-solitaire-strategy/) shows how to keep columns open and avoid tangles.

### [FreeCell](/freecell/)

Every card is on the table from the start, so it's pure planning — and almost every deal is winnable. If you like a puzzle you can actually solve with thought rather than luck, this is your game. See the [FreeCell strategy guide](/guides/freecell-strategy/).

### Quick rounds: [Pyramid](/pyramid/), [TriPeaks](/tripeaks/) &amp; [Golf](/golf/)

Three lighter solitaires for a short break. Pyramid has you pairing cards that sum to thirteen; TriPeaks rewards long streaks as you clear three peaks; Golf is a fast "play up or down" race to empty the course. Perfect for a few minutes between tasks.

## Puzzles

### [Sudoku](/sudoku/)

Pure logic across four difficulties, with pencil marks built in. Every CardHearth puzzle is guaranteed solvable without guessing. Learn the full ladder of methods in our [Sudoku techniques guide](/guides/sudoku-solving-techniques/).

### [Mahjong Solitaire](/mahjong/)

Match free pairs of tiles to dismantle the layout. Every CardHearth deal is built to be solvable, so a stuck board is a puzzle to read — not a dead end you were handed.

### [Minesweeper](/minesweeper/) &amp; [2048](/2048/)

Two modern classics. Minesweeper is a game of deduction — read the numbers, flag the mines, and (on CardHearth) take back a misclick with undo. 2048 is the addictive slide-and-merge tile chase. Both are one click away, free.

## A new game every day

Want variety without choosing? The [Daily Challenge](/daily-challenge/) serves one game each day on a fixed rotation — Sudoku, Mahjong, FreeCell and 2048, the same for everyone — and tracks your streak. It's the easiest way to keep a daily habit and discover games you might not have tried.
